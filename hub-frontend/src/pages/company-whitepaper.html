XERA

The token that ties it together

XERA works with BNB Smart Chain; it is not an independent blockchain. Inside our apps your balance is called Pure XERA: you earn it by taking part, send it to other XERA users, and use it for discounts. When you withdraw it to your own wallet it becomes XERA, the on-chain token. The XERA token is deployed on BNB Smart Chain mainnet. Moving Pure XERA on-chain (claiming) is not yet open to everyone, and its opening is announced in the app. The full token details are in the XERA whitepaper, and what is and is not promised is in the XERA disclosure.

Discounts

Why we offer discounts, and how they work

A token is only useful if you can do something with it. Discounts are the simplest everyday use of XERA: you apply some of your Pure XERA to a purchase, and you pay less in cedis.

Why discounts

Real use for XERA

Discounts give XERA a practical job inside the ecosystem, beyond holding it.

Reward participation

People who mine, invite friends and complete verification have something useful to spend their Pure XERA on.

Better value for customers

Customers pay less for the same product, and have a reason to come back.

Transparent by design

Every use is recorded in the XERA ledger, so it can be checked and reconciled.

How a discount works

  1. You earn Pure XERA in the XERA app through mining, rewards and referrals.
  2. At checkout in a product that offers discounts, you choose to use Pure XERA.
  3. Before you confirm, you see exactly how much XERA will be used and how much you save.
  4. When you start to pay, that Pure XERA is held for your order so it cannot be spent twice.
  5. Once the payment is confirmed, the hold becomes final and is recorded in the discount vault. If the payment never completes, the hold is released and nothing is deducted.

Ground rules

Set per product

Each product publishes its own discount rate and limit. The discount is capped as a share of the price, and rates can change, so always check the figures shown at checkout.

Only spendable balance

You can only use Pure XERA that is free to spend. Amounts held for an order that is still pending cannot be used twice.

Verified accounts

Accounts that have completed identity verification use less XERA for the same discount. Accounts that have not can still use discounts, but need more XERA for the same saving.

Not cash, not a promise

A discount is a benefit on a purchase. It is not a cash payout, a price guarantee or an investment return.
