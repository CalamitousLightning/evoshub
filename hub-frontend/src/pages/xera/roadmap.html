Roadmap

We only say something is live when you can use it today. Nothing here is a promise of a launch date. See the disclosure page for more.

Live now

  • Your XERA account
  • Your wallet, with your balance
  • Mining sessions that run for 24 hours
  • Daily claims, with a streak that grows each day
  • Your full history of every transaction
  • The XERA token and its mining, vesting and distribution contracts on BNB Smart Chain (details)
  • Discounts on EVOS Data when you pay with XERA
  • A directory of the other EVOXERA products

Being built

  • Moving XERA to your own wallet, opening in stages. You need to verify your identity (KYC), pay a one-time processing fee, and cover the small BNB network fee
  • More public pages, such as statistics and token details
  • Links with new EVOXERA products as they launch

Ideas we are thinking about

  • Buying and selling XERA
  • Sending XERA to other users inside the app
  • Listing XERA on an exchange or marketplace
  • Other blockchains, including TON
  • Rewards for inviting friends

These are ideas, not promises. If we build any of them, it will depend on our planning and on the rules that apply to us.
